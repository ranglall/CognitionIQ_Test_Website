The Process

How It Works

From baseline to recommendation in four simple steps.

  1. 01

    Baseline assessment

    Participants complete our 5–10 minute assessment using a VR headset, following visual targets with their eyes.

  2. 02

    Secure storage

    Their cognitive data is securely stored and accessible through your organization's CognitionIQ portal.

  3. 03

    Retest after an incident

    If a potential injury occurs, the participant retakes the same 5–10 minute assessment.

  4. 04

    Clear recommendation

    Our specialized algorithm compares both tests and delivers clear return-to-play recommendations to your portal.

The entire process is

  • Standardized
  • Objective
  • Fast
