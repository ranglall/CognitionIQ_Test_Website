Our Technology

All-new technology

At the core of our platform is a fully patented oculometric assessment algorithm. By precisely measuring how the eyes move, it captures objective markers of cognitive function quickly, non-invasively, and without relying on subjective questionnaires.

  • Patented algorithm

    A proprietary oculometric method protected by patent.

  • 5–10 minute assessment

    Fast enough to fit into a routine visit.

  • Objective measurement

    Data-driven results based on eye-movement behaviour.

The Process

How It Works

A simple, three-step process from assessment to insight.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Participants complete our 5–10 minute assessment using a headset with built-in eye tracking.

  2. 02

    Analyze

    Their eye-movement data is securely stored and analyzed by our patented oculometric algorithm.

  3. 03

    Report

    Clear, objective results are delivered to support informed decisions about brain health.
